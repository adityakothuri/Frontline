## What Frontline is

Frontline is a **resource navigator** for U.S. military veterans. It helps you find benefits
programs, local organizations, and practical next steps, and it uses AI to explain them in plain
English.

## What Frontline is not

- **Frontline is not the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs**, any government agency, or any
  official representative of one. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the VA.
- **Frontline does not provide legal, medical, financial, or benefits advice.** Summaries, next-step
  plans, and call scripts are AI-generated, clearly labeled, simplified guidance — not professional
  advice and not a determination of your eligibility for anything.
- **Frontline does not file claims, submit applications, or communicate with any agency on your
  behalf.**
- **Frontline is not an emergency service.** If you are in crisis, call or text **988 and press 1**
  (Veterans Crisis Line), or call **911**.

## Accuracy, and why you must verify

Resource information is ingested from official and nonprofit sources and is shown with its source
and a last-checked date. Even so, **details change**: hours shift, programs close, eligibility rules
are updated, and third-party directories go stale. AI-generated text can also be wrong or
incomplete.

**Always confirm details directly with the organization or the official source before you rely on
them, travel anywhere, or make a decision.** Frontline is provided "as is," without warranties of
any kind, and we do not guarantee that any resource is currently available, accurate, or right for
your situation.
