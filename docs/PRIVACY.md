# Frontline — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-26_

Frontline helps U.S. veterans find benefits and local resources. We designed it to collect **as
little as possible**. This policy explains what the app handles and why. Plain version: **there is no
account, and we never ask for your SSN, VA login, bank information, or medical diagnosis.**

## What we collect and where it lives

- **Optional profile (on your device).** A first name and ZIP code you may enter to personalize
  results. This is stored **locally on your device**, not on our servers, and you can clear it any time
  in the app (Profile → Reset).
- **Location (optional).** If you allow it, your device location is used **only** to center the map on
  you. It is not stored or shared. You can instead just type a ZIP.
- **Anonymous device backup (optional).** So your saved resources and profile survive a reinstall, the
  app can back them up keyed to a **random, anonymous device identifier** — not your name, email, or
  account. There is no way to tie this identifier to your identity.

## What we send to our services

- **Resource search.** Your ZIP or approximate location is sent to our backend to return nearby
  resources. We do not build a profile of you from these searches.
- **AI guidance.** When you open a resource, the app can generate a plain-English summary, a next-step
  plan, and a call script. To do this, information **about that resource** (and, if you provide it, your
  first name for a personalized call script) is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) through a secure
  server. We do not send your ZIP, location history, or any sensitive personal data for this.
- **Crash reports.** If the app crashes, a report is sent to our error-monitoring provider (Sentry)
  so we can fix it. These reports contain the technical error, your device model and OS version, and
  the screen you were on — **not** your name, ZIP, location, or anything you typed. We do not use
  them to identify or track you.
- Network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## What we do NOT do

- No user accounts, logins, or passwords.
- No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party trackers, no selling of data.
- We never request or store SSN, VA credentials, financial information, or health diagnoses.

## AI and accuracy

Frontline is a **resource navigator, not the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or any government
agency**, and it is not affiliated with them. AI-generated summaries, plans, and scripts are
**simplified guidance, clearly labeled, and are not legal, medical, financial, or
government-certified advice.** Always confirm details with the official source.

## Children

Frontline is intended for adults (veterans and those helping them) and is not directed at children
under 13.

## Data retention & your choices

- On-device data (profile, saved items) stays until you clear it or uninstall the app.
- The anonymous backup can be discarded by resetting your profile and saved list in the app.
- Because there is no account, we cannot look up "your" data by name — it is only reachable from your
  device.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be
reflected in the app's store listing.
