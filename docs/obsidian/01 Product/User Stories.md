# User Stories

Written as `As a <user>, I want <goal>, so that <reason>.` MVP stories are marked ✅. See
[[User Personas]] for who these users are.

## Entry & guided need flow (core)

- ✅ As a veteran, I want a calm home that asks **"What do you need help with?"**, so that I know
  exactly where to start without reading instructions.
- ✅ As a veteran, I want to pick a **need category** (food, housing, healthcare, benefits, …), so
  that the app focuses on my actual situation.
- ✅ As a veteran, I want a few **short guided questions** (what, how urgent, in-person or online,
  what documents I have, what kind of help I want), so that the results and next steps fit me.
- ✅ As a veteran, I want to enter a **ZIP code** if I don't want to share exact location, so that I
  stay in control of my privacy.

## Discovery: list & map (core)

- ✅ As a veteran, I want to see **nearby resources** as **cards** with the most important info
  (name, category, distance, open/closed, best next action), so that I can scan fast.
- ✅ As a veteran, I want to see the same resources on a **map**, so that I can judge what's actually
  close and reachable.

## Resource detail, summaries & next steps (core)

- ✅ As a veteran, I want a **plain-English summary** of a benefit/resource (what it is, who it
  helps, who may qualify, documents, deadlines, what to do next), so that I don't have to decode
  bureaucratic pages.
- ✅ As a veteran, I want to see **what documents to bring** as a checklist, so that I don't get
  turned away.
- ✅ As a veteran, I want **AI-generated next steps** for a resource, so that I know who to call and
  in what order.
- ✅ As a veteran, I want the app to be **honest that it's guidance, not the VA or legal advice**, so
  that I trust it and verify with official sources.

## Call scripts (core)

- ✅ As a veteran, I want a **call script** tailored to the resource and category, so that I know
  what to say on the phone.
- ✅ As a nervous veteran, I want a **shorter, simpler script**, so that I can ask for help without
  feeling overwhelmed.

## Save & profile

- ✅ As a veteran, I want to **save** resources, benefits, scripts, and document checklists, so that
  I can come back to them later.
- ✅ As a veteran, I want a **lightweight profile** (ZIP, who I am, needs, radius, documents I have)
  that's **optional**, so that recommendations improve without the app demanding sensitive data.

## Trust & safety (core)

- ✅ As a veteran, I want each resource to show **where the info came from and when it was last
  checked**, so that I know how much to trust it.
- ✅ As a veteran, I want a **warning when info may be outdated or unverified**, so that I confirm
  before relying on it.

## Later (not MVP)
